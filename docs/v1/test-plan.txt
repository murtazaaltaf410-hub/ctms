Field test on a real bus

Run this once on staging in week 3 and again on production before the pilot starts. Use at
least two student phones (one Android model of each brand you can borrow) and the real
driver phone.

Before the ride

     Driver app installed, signed in with the driver's phone number, battery optimisation
     disabled for the app
     Student phones signed in, alert mode on, location set to "always" and notifications
     allowed
     Route and stops created; polyline looks right on the map
     Server awake and Google Maps keys restricted

During the ride

     Trip starts in two taps and the bus appears for students within 5 seconds
     Marker follows the real bus without jumping, including under trees and near buildings
     Status "Delayed" appears on student phones
     Student standing still gets one alert as the bus approaches, at roughly 500 m
     Student on the bus gets no alert
     Driver switches the phone to airplane mode for a minute, then back; map catches up
     Lock the driver's phone for 10 minutes; uploads continue
     ETA at a stop is compared with the real arrival time and the error is noted
After the ride
     Trip ends and the report shows distance and times close to the real values
     Battery use on the driver phone over the ride is recorded
     Dead spots, wrong alerts and slow updates are written down with time and place

Pilot acceptance and exit criteria

The pilot starts only when every launch item passes, and it is judged a success against the
PRD's success metrics after two weeks of use.
Launch gate (all must pass)

     TC1 to TC20 pass on staging, with no open high-severity bug
     Field test completed on the real bus with no blocking issue
     Driver completes a full trip using only the written onboarding steps
     Admin creates a route, adds a stop, posts an announcement and exports a trip report
     without help
     Google Maps quotas and budget alerts configured; keys restricted
     Privacy notice and location consent screens approved by the university contact
     Rollback plan from the runbook rehearsed once
Pilot success (after two weeks)
     At least 80% of active trip time has a fix under 10 seconds old
     Median ETA error under 2 minutes
     Nearby alerts arrive before the bus passes in at least 90% of tested cases
     At least 30 students installed and used the app
     No unresolved privacy complaint
When a metric misses, record the cause (hardware, network, algorithm or adoption) and
the fix before widening the pilot.
